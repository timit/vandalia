## corrections and superseded ideas

- SOK discharge current was recorded wrong: parts.md and README said 200A continuous/500A peak; the SOK's own manual says 150A continuous, 170A@60s, 800A@10s peak. corrected in both files.
- SOK charge voltage: this build targets absorption ≤27.6V on the XS and multiplus, slightly below the SOK's own spec (28V charge / 27.6V float) — a deliberate margin, not a correction to either figure.
- no 70A ANL fuse exists. use the 60A (5123) on the XS output.
- orion-tr smart 12/24-15 replaced by orion XS 1400.
- second generator inlet dropped. the generator plugs into the shore inlet.
- ANL block studs are 5/16" (M8), not 3/8".
- DJI SDC output was recorded wrong: parts.md/README said 12A per port. DJI's own spec for the SDC-to-XT60 cable (the one feeding the 5026 bus) is 13.6V default, 10A max. the 40A ANL fuse already on that feed is oversized for the cable and should be closer to 10A — flagged, not yet changed.
- earlier "no ground bar in the 3131" was an inference. the instructions mention ring terminals for grounding connections.
- superseded 2026-09-21: **pure** direct-DC velit feed (24V bank straight to the velit's 24V DC input, with no other source at all) is still not adopted — it would lose the DJI backup path every other major load has via its own selector switch. but a **hybrid** version is now adopted: battery selector 2 gives the velit a direct 24V bus tap on one leg and the DJI-fed meanwell on the other, so it keeps dual-source redundancy *and* recovers the inverter+meanwell round-trip conversion loss (~17%, from the multiplus's 94% max inverter efficiency and the meanwell's 88% typical efficiency — see the 2026-09-21 change log entry) whenever it runs on battery power. this replaces the BAOMAIN-2-to-meanwell design below.
- not adopted: ATS, smartshunt.
- 5026 feed fuse was the wrong format, not just oversized: a 40A ANL was specified for a cable rated 13.6V/10A max, but ANL fuses aren't made that small (typically starting around 30A) — the fix is a 10-15A ATC/ATO inline fuse (5065-style holder), not just a smaller ANL.
- the multiplus's factory default battery profile (24V: 28.8V absorption, 27.6V float) is not what this build uses — it must be changed to a lithium profile at or below 27.6V absorption before charging the SOK. see wiring.md settings.
- not adopted: replacing BAOMAIN 3 with a direct 8077-to-multiplus feed plus a separate 15A breaker to the DJI. loses the interlock (nothing then stops both systems drawing from the shore feed at once, which can overload the honda or trip the 8077 main) and the neutral isolation (BAOMAIN 3 breaks hot and neutral together; a single-pole breaker would leave the DJI's neutral permanently tied to the shared bus).
- dropped the separate 4 AWG / 80A ANL (5124) van-feed-to-XS run. the DJI charger's already-installed wire and 100A fuse is the shared source for both the XS and the DJI charger, through battery selector 1. the 5124 is no longer needed.
- the cerbo power plan (5065 holder, 3A ATC fuse, #8 rings) was wrong. the cerbo's supplied cable already has an inline 3.15A slow-blow fuse and M8 ring eyes built in — nothing to add. dropped 5065/3A ATC from the shopping list.
- never power the cerbo from a multiplus AC-out — victron's manual warns this can deadlock the system after a fault (neither device can restart the other).
